Everything Your Team Needs

Combining Google Meet's simplicity, Zoom's rich meeting controls, and Discord's collaborative workspace spirit.

1. Video Meetings

Ultra-low latency browser-based peer-to-peer WebRTC streaming with automatic active-speaker detection and audio level meters.

2. Screen Sharing

Share your full desktop, application window, or browser tab with full HD resolution and synchronized participant spotlight tiles.

3. Real-Time Chat

Side-panel instant messaging delivered reliably over Django Channels WebSockets with persistent message history and emoji reactions.

4. File Sharing

Securely upload and distribute meeting assets (PDFs, presentations, code, images, zip files) with strictly authorized access control.

5. Collaborative Whiteboard

Multi-user synchronized HTML5 Canvas whiteboard equipped with pen, highlighters, geometric shapes, arrows, text, and undo/redo.

6. Secure Meetings

Protected by Django session authentication, CSRF validation, meeting access control, host permissions, and waiting room support.
